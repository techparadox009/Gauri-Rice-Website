Since 1960

Naya Bazar, Lahori Gate

Our corporate office sits inside Asia's largest grain market — meaning we're approached by, and have direct access to, practically every serious rice buyer across India.

Shrejal Impex Pvt. Ltd. is the leading supplier of 1121 Basmati Long Grain Steam Rice in India. The foundation of our business has always been simple: delight the customer with the quality of the product. Today our rice is sold and cooked over more than 5 lakh sale counters, restaurants and hotels across Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, Jammu & Kashmir, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Uttarakhand, Bihar and more.

We're a one-stop solution for every kind of rice — Steam, Parboiled, Raw and Brown — across varieties including Basmati, 1121 Long Grain Basmati, 1509 Long Grain, 1401, Sugandha, PUSA PR11, Sarbati, Permal, DP and Sona Mussorrie. Our procurement professionals carry 50+ years of combined experience sourcing paddy, blending seasoned expertise with fresh talent to keep supply uninterrupted, whatever the season.

"Yesterday, today and future is Consistent Quality."

Milestones

From a grain-market stall to a six-brand house.

1960

Trading begins in Old Delhi

The founding generation starts trading rice from Naya Bazar, Lahori Gate — inside what would become Asia's largest grain market.

2011

Incorporated as Shrejal Impex Pvt. Ltd.

The business is formally incorporated, structuring five decades of trading relationships into a professional supply operation.

Since

GT Karnal Road processing plant

A 1,55,000 sq ft facility comes online — 600 MT/day production capacity and 20,000 MT of storage, run to a zero-defect standard.

Today

Six brands, thirteen-plus states

Gauri, ST, Saira Banu, Zara, Era and Shahi Bharat reach more than 5 lakh counters, restaurants and hotels nationwide.
