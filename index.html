Our story

A trading legacy from Old Delhi's grain heart.

Founded in 1960 and formally incorporated as Shrejal Impex Pvt. Ltd., our corporate office sits in Naya Bazar, Lahori Gate — Asia's largest grain market — giving us direct access to India's finest paddy and rice buyers alike. Six decades on, that foundation is still what "delighting the customer" is built on.

  • One-stop supplier: Steam, Parboiled, Raw & Brown rice
  • Procurement team with 50+ years combined experience
  • Varieties spanning 1121, 1509, 1401, Sugandha, Sona Mussorrie & more
