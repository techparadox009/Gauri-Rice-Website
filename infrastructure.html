One team, one motive

Best quality rice. No exceptions.

Every team at Shrejal Impex Pvt. Ltd. works toward the same goal — rice that reaches the customer as the best quality rice possible. Our quality and processing team reports on moisture, foreign matter, percentage of discolouration, aroma, length and stickiness, all after proper lab testing of every prospective purchase.

Only once that lab test clears does processing begin. From there, rice runs through a series of machines that strip out dust, thread, glass, metal, plastic granules, damaged rice, immature grain and discoloured grain — under the controlled supervision of highly skilled manpower, start to finish.

The process

From paddy to packed sack, four stages.

Stage 01

Lab Testing

Moisture, colour, aroma, length and stickiness are checked before any purchase is accepted for processing.

Stage 02

Cleaning & De-stoning

Pre-Fine Cleaners and De-stoners with Turbo Air Extraction remove dust, stone, glass, metal and plastic granules.

Stage 03

Sizing & Grading

Precision-Sizers and Length Graders sort every grain to a consistent size and length specification.

Stage 04

Sorting & Packing

A Bühler "Z Series" rice sorter enforces our zero-defect policy before the final sack is sealed.
